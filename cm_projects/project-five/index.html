Air Calligraphy

Write in the air with your index finger. Slow moves make thick brush strokes, quick flicks make thin ones, and symmetry mode turns your hand into a rangoli maker.

Mouse mode: drag on the canvas to draw.

Hand mode gestures: point with your index finger, with the other fingers folded, to draw. Open your whole hand, or make a fist, to lift the pen. Keep your hand inside the camera view and well lit. Camera pictures stay in your browser and are never uploaded. The camera needs a secure (https) page, which GitHub Pages provides.
