Game of Life Sonifier

Draw live cells, press Play, and hear the pattern. A playhead sweeps left to right; every live cell it touches plays a note.

How to play: click or drag on the grid to add or remove cells. Rows are pitches (higher up is higher). Columns are time. Cells with 2 or 3 neighbours survive, and an empty cell with exactly 3 neighbours is born, so the music keeps changing by itself. Sound starts after you press Play, because browsers need a click first.
