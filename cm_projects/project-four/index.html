Heritage Hunt

Three puzzles in the old city of Hyderabad. Solve each one to unlock facts for your journal.

Level 1: Rebuild the Charminar

Slide the tiles into place to rebuild the picture. Click a tile next to the gap.

Level 2: Hunt in Golconda Fort

Five things are hidden in this scene: a gem, the gate’s iron spikes, a cannon, the highest pavilion, and the spot where a clap echoes. Click to find them.

Level 3: Put history in order

Use the arrows to arrange these events from earliest to latest.

Heritage journal
Solve a puzzle to add your first fact.

Facts are drawn from public sources on Charminar and Golconda Fort. Dates and figures differ between sources, so check with the Telangana Tourism and ASI pages before using them in class.
