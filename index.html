Dr. Minhaj Abdullah: Growing stories, one frame at a time.

Assistant Professor of Animation, VFX, AI & Computational Media, School of Digital Media and Communication, Mahindra University, Hyderabad, India.

I teach, research and direct films where animation meets generative AI and immersive media, after twenty years in studios and classrooms. I teach Animation and VFX, Computer Graphics, Generative AI, Computational Media and AR/VR, and run the AR/VR and Unreal Engine Laboratory. My AI-driven and immersive short films have won Best Short Film awards at national and international festivals, and I have published journal papers and Scopus-indexed book chapters on AI, AR/VR and digital twins in education.

  • Research: papers and book chapters on generative AI, AR/VR and 3D learning
  • Awards: festival wins and selections, short films
  • Teaching: courses, classroom slides, textbooks and online tutorials
  • Projects: student films, immersive builds and videos
  • Gallery: paintings, sketches and digital art
